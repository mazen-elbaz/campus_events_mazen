Campus Hack Day 2026

About the event

A full day of building. Form a team of up to four, pick a track, and turn an idea into a working prototype. Mentors from the university and local companies will review your progress and judge the final demos.

  • Date: Saturday, 14 November 2026
  • Time: 10:00 – 18:00
  • Venue: Innovation Hall, Main Campus
  • Category: Technology
